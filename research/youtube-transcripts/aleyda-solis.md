## Video 1: AI Search Crawlability & Technical Foundations

**Date:** January 13, 2026  
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pqrwpXpMM6s  
**Channel:** Humans of Martech Podcast  
**Duration:** 58 minutes  
**Show notes:** https://humansofmartech.com/202-aleyda-solis-ai-search-crawlability/

### Key Insights

- AI crawlers behave differently from Googlebot—they largely consume raw HTML and **do not render JavaScript**, so client-side content is invisible to them.
- Hosting/CDN rules often block AI user agents while allowing Googlebot; teams must verify access at the infrastructure layer, not just robots.txt.
- LLMs are a new discovery channel alongside Google, TikTok, Instagram, and other platforms—SEO must become multi-platform.
- Generic TOFU content collapses in AI Overviews; brands need **brand-led informational content** (research, case studies, FAQs, help centers) with specificity AI cannot replicate.
- Topic-level analysis beats prompt-level chasing—cluster prompts by topic, track competitors and sentiment within each cluster.
- Community signals (Reddit, Discord, YouTube comments, forums) now shape LLM authority faster than traditional backlink profiles.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

This is the most actionable video for content teams. Aleyda explicitly addresses what content to create for AI search: not keyword-matched explainers, but original research, case studies, competitive breakdowns, and help-center resources rooted in real customer language. She warns against using AI to scale generic TOFU content that AI systems will summarize without sending clicks.

- LLMs evaluate brand trust through entity consistency, third-party corroboration, and community proof—not just on-page SEO signals.

## Video 3: The AI Search Optimization Roadmap

**Date:** September 10, 2025  
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BjyF_4UhoOM  
**Channel:** Dennis Shiao  
**Duration:** 1 hour, 3 minutes  
**Note:** Slightly outside the 6-month window but included as a foundational roadmap reference.

### Key Insights

- **AI search vs. traditional search — 3 differences:** probabilistic vs. deterministic clicks, decision-engine vs. retrieval system, conversational prompts vs. keyword queries.
- **4-step roadmap for AI content optimization:**
  1. Research and assess AI search audience behavior
  2. Optimize for content crawlability and indexability
  3. Establish business-aligned topical authority
  4. Optimize for chunk-level retrieval (passage-level, not page-level)
- Investment in AI search should be proportional to how much your audience already uses LLM platforms.
- Chunk-level retrieval means content must be structured in extractable, self-contained passages—not long unstructured pages.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

The "chunk-level retrieval" step directly informs AI content production workflows: every section of content must stand alone as an extractable answer. This changes how teams use AI writing tools—they must enforce structural templates (H2 = question, first paragraph = direct answer, supporting evidence below) rather than generating long-form prose.
