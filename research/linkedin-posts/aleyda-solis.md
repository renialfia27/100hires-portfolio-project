## Post 1: The New Rules of AI Visibility (Moz Guide)

**Date:** ~May 24, 2026 (posted 3 weeks before June 14, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aleyda_my-moz-guide-based-on-my-recent-webinar-activity-7463253507266052097-FXwm  
**Related resource:** https://moz.com/blog/rules-of-ai-visibility

### Key Insights

- AI search **expands** the search journey; it does not replace SEO fundamentals (crawlability, indexability, helpful content, entity consistency, authority).
- Teams should avoid two extremes: treating SEO as dead, or pretending nothing has changed.
- Success metrics must evolve beyond traffic: surface visibility, citations, recommendations, accurate representation, comparison wins, and whether the right pages/offers are used.
- Introduces **10 brand characteristics** for AI search readiness: Accessible, Useful, Extractable, Recognizable, Consistent, Corroborated, Credible, Differentiated, Fresh, and Transactable.
- Prioritization should follow visibility gaps: invisible → visible but not recommended → recommended but misrepresented → visible without measurable value.
- SEO, digital PR, brand, social, community, and reputation management must work together.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

This post is a foundational framework for AI content strategy. It defines what “good” AI-ready content looks like before production begins—content must be extractable, corroborated, and differentiated, not just keyword-optimized. For 100hires research, it establishes that AI content production should start with readiness audits, not volume.

## Post 2: How AI Systems Surface Your Brand (Inputs → Outcomes)

**Date:** ~April 14, 2026 (posted 2 months before June 14, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aleyda_how-ai-systems-surface-your-brandfrom-activity-7444484026897354753-sgDm

### Key Insights

- AI systems need three **inputs** before surfacing a brand: **Access** (can reach/process content), **Usefulness** (content worth reusing), and **Recognition** (signals connecting content to a credible brand).
- Four **outcomes** to measure: **Presence** (do you show up?), **Position** (how prominent?), **Perception** (how are you described?), and **Influence** (do you shape the answer?).
- Recognition is the most underestimated input—it requires consistent, unambiguous entity signals across sources AI systems actually ingest, not just mention volume.
- Influence is the highest level: when your brand’s data, concepts, or frameworks shape how a category is explained.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

Directly maps content production to AI retrieval mechanics. Content teams must produce structurally useful, entity-consistent assets—not generic AI drafts. The Access/Usefulness/Recognition model gives a QA checklist for every piece of AI-assisted content before publication.

## Post 4: Ecommerce AI Search — Evidence-to-Click Layer

**Date:** ~May 24, 2026 (posted 3 weeks before June 14, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aleyda_ecommerce-ai-search-trends-wins-how-activity-7463497689671065600-qAkS

### Key Insights

- Introduces the **“evidence-to-click layer”**: AI consumes proof (reviews, specs, third-party validation) inside the answer before users click.
- **Query-to-page targeting breaks down** as prompts decompose into sub-queries; pages must cover sub-query sets, not just primary keywords.
- Content that ranks ≠ content AI trusts enough to cite; provenance signals (real-world testing, corroborated expertise, entity consistency) matter more than generic SEO artifacts.
- Ecommerce brands must optimize off-site evidence (G2, comparison pages, integration docs) as much as on-page content—the buyer arrives “half-decided.”

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

Critical for AI content at scale: production must cover **topic completeness** and **sub-query clusters**, not single-keyword pages. AI-generated content without third-party corroboration will rank but won’t be cited. This reshapes content briefs from “target keyword X” to “cover sub-query set Y with extractable evidence.”

## Post 5: ChatGPT Link Prominence — Traffic & Engagement Data

**Date:** ~May 24, 2026 (posted 3 weeks before June 14, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aleyda_more-traffic-and-engagement-insights-about-activity-7463634692743925760-Xiqj

### Key Insights

- After ChatGPT’s May 7, 2026 changes giving links more prominence (Similarweb data):
  - Referral visits from ChatGPT increased **~150%**
  - **~60%** of referral traffic lands on brand homepages
  - **+24%** uplift in pageviews per visit; **+11%** uplift in time on site
- ChatGPT referral traffic is **brand-led**, not generic discovery—users arrive with the brand already in mind.
- Homepage experience and brand clarity matter more than landing-page optimization for AI referral traffic.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

Shows AI search is becoming a **real traffic channel**, not just a visibility metric. Content production must ensure homepages and core brand pages are clear, structured, and machine-readable—because that’s where AI sends users. AI content strategies should prioritize brand-level clarity, not just long-tail blog posts.
