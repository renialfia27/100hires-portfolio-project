## Post 2: AI Is the Death of Google Search — Data and Remedy

**Date:** ~July 2025 (posted 11 months before June 16, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ruben-hassid_ai-is-the-death-of-google-search-heres-activity-7340694737550503938-B_5L

### Key Insights

- **58.5%** of U.S. Google queries ended without a click in 2024; AI Overviews cut click-through by ~30%.
- Only **one in five** visits to top-100 sites now starts on a search engine; publisher traffic from Google is declining sharply.
- ChatGPT hit **500M weekly active users**; Perplexity logged **780M queries** in May 2025—AI is winning discovery share.
- Remedy: upload Google Analytics CSV to ChatGPT and identify URLs with >40% Google/organic dependency as **RISK**.
- Tools like **PromptWatch** help monitor LLM visibility; rewrite or create content targeting AI citation gaps.
- Search isn’t dying—it’s **shifting** from destination to conversation.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

Provides a diagnostic workflow for content teams: audit organic dependency, identify at-risk URLs, and **prioritize AI-optimized rewrites** for high-risk pages. Production should include LLM visibility monitoring alongside traditional rank tracking.

## Post 3: The One Prompt to Make ChatGPT Write Naturally

**Date:** ~May 2025 (posted 13 months before June 16, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ruben-hassid_the-one-prompt-to-make-chatgpt-write-naturally-activity-7339879340999888896-zxiE

### Key Insights

- AI-generated content fails when it uses giveaway phrases (“let’s dive in,” “game-changing,” “unleash potential”).
- Eight-step prompt framework: plain language → avoid clichés → be direct → natural tone → no marketing buzzwords → be honest → simplify grammar → remove fluff.
- Goal is **clear, simple, authentic writing** that resonates with real people—not polished AI slop.
- Example: “We should leverage this opportunity” becomes “Let’s use this chance.”
- Conversational flow (starting sentences with “and” or “but”) beats robotic perfection.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

A practical **AI content QA prompt** for production pipelines. Every AI draft should pass through humanization guidelines that strip AI clichés and marketing fluff—essential for content that ranks in both Google and LLM answers without triggering quality penalties.

## Post 4: How to Search with AI in May 2025

**Date:** ~June 2025 (posted 12 months before June 16, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ruben-hassid_you-cant-search-without-using-ai-here-activity-7332631576087805953-qYRu

### Key Insights

- **ChatGPT + o3 + search** behaves like an agent scrolling the web—best for multi-step research prompts.
- **Grok + DeeperSearch** excels at real-time news and social signal research.
- **Perplexity + Research mode** creates full cited reports from live sources.
- **ChatGPT Deep Research** produces 10–20 page diligence reports with APA citations.
- Multi-tool research workflows (Perplexity → ChatGPT critique → Kagi PDF search → extraction) replace single Google queries.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

Defines the **research stack** for AI content production. Content teams should use agentic search (not just Google) for brief research, competitor analysis, and fact-gathering before writing—mirroring how LLMs themselves discover and cite sources.
