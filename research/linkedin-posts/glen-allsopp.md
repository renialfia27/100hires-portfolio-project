## Post 2: NerdWallet CEO Confirms AI Overviews Cutting Search Traffic

**Date:** ~May 2025 (posted 13 months before June 16, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/glen-allsopp-63084025_nerdwallet-ceo-tim-chen-just-confirmed-ai-activity-7325903341027241985-dgQS

### Key Insights

- NerdWallet Q1 2025: **$209M revenue (+29% YoY)** despite **-29% monthly unique users**—AI Overviews cutting search traffic while monetization adapts.
- Insurance grew **246% YoY**; banking +22%; credit card revenue declined **24%** to $38M.
- CEO Tim Chen cited **“organic search headwinds”** from AI with outsized effect on credit card vertical.
- Search traffic stabilizing; hopes for traffic growth resuming in 2026.
- Even dominant finance content brands face AI-driven traffic erosion—no vertical is immune.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

Case study proving AI traffic loss doesn’t automatically kill revenue—but content production must **shift toward higher-converting verticals and formats** that survive AI compression. Finance/review content teams need AI citation strategies, not just traffic volume targets.
