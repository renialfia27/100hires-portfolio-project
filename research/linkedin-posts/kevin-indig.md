## Post 1: 11 Levers for AEO — Retrieved → Cited → Trusted

**Date:** ~April 2026 (posted 2 months before June 16, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_same-tactics-new-game-if-you-think-ai-activity-7414334371656245248-O-sB

### Key Insights

- AEO doesn’t require a new playbook—traditional SEO is foundational; the pipeline upgrades from **Crawl → Index → Rank** to **Retrieved → Cited → Trusted**.
- **Stage 1 — Retrieved**: selection rate/primary bias, <200ms TTFB, metadata optimized for machine parsing, product feeds for transactional queries.
- **Stage 2 — Cited**: semantic HTML with high fact density, FAQ coverage mirroring conversational prompts, content freshness (<3 months), third-party mentions, top-10 organic position (~0.65 correlation with AI mentions).
- **Stage 3 — Trusted**: demonstrated expertise via credentials/bylines, UGC presence on Reddit and YouTube for answer validation.
- By 2026, over 50% of searches will be AI-influenced—teams must measure and optimize each lever.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

The most comprehensive production checklist in this research set. Every content piece should be tagged against all 11 levers—especially **fact density, FAQ formatting, freshness, and third-party validation**. Production SLAs should include 90-day refresh cycles for AI-critical content.

## Post 2: SEO Is No Longer About Keywords

**Date:** ~February 2025 (posted 16 months before June 16, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_seo-is-no-longer-about-keywords-far-from-activity-7299065429241614336-3ZMO

### Key Insights

- Traditional keyword SEO died years ago—SERP features, AI Overviews in 100+ countries, and ads throughout results killed click value from rankings alone.
- Example: ranking #1 for “in-house SEO” (200+ monthly searches) produced **zero clicks**.
- 2025 pivot: focus on **user intent** over keywords, target high-attention SERP features, leverage YouTube and social platforms.
- Use paid search data and Reddit/social behavior analysis for content direction.
- Future prediction: “SEO” for LLMs by **reverse-engineering top AI results**—just as was done with Google.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

Shifts content briefs from keyword targets to **intent clusters and platform visibility**. Production should prioritize YouTube scripts, Reddit participation, and SERP feature optimization alongside written content—rankings without clicks are worthless.

## Post 3: Google’s AI Overviews — Data vs. Narrative

**Date:** ~June 2025 (posted 12 months before June 16, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_google-claims-ai-overviews-are-revolutionizing-activity-7322640525562400769-U8Fx  
**Related resource:** https://www.growth-memo.com/

### Key Insights

- Google claims AI Overviews drive longer, more complex queries—but Similarweb data (5B+ queries) shows query length grew only **3%** (3.27 → 3.37 words) over two years.
- Users visit Google more frequently (+9% US) but spend **less time per visit**—“speed dating” not deep engagement.
- UK metrics stayed flat despite identical AI Overview technology—regional differences expose narrative contradictions.
- New reality for marketers: **“visit more, stay less”**—quality of each site visit matters more than volume.
- AI Overviews create quick-answer behavior, not deeper search engagement as Google claims.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

Content must deliver **immediate value on landing** because AI sends fewer but higher-intent visitors. Production should optimize above-the-fold clarity, fast value delivery, and conversion paths—not long introductory fluff that assumes exploratory browsing.

## Post 4: Google Search Console Measurement Gap

**Date:** ~April 2026 (posted 2 months before June 16, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_we-often-treat-google-search-console-as-the-activity-7424084813181104129-YA4X

### Key Insights

- GSC is shifting from comprehensive reporting to a **directional signal**—~75% of impressions filtered for privacy, ~38% of clicks missing from reports.
- Bot/scraper activity increased **25%** in 6 months, potentially skewing remaining data.
- AI Overview rollout (March 2025) and search parameter updates fundamentally changed the measurement landscape.
- Adaptation: **triangulate** GSC with server logs and third-party rank trackers; adjust forecasting for privacy gap; implement active bot filtering.
- Best SEO teams optimize **how they measure**, not just what they publish.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

Content ROI reporting must use **multiple data sources**—GSC alone underreports AI-era performance. Production teams should integrate server log analysis and third-party AI visibility tools into content performance dashboards.

## Post 5: SEO Graduating from Clicks to Influence

**Date:** ~June 2025 (posted 12 months before June 16, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_constant-change-is-the-new-normal-for-search-activity-7340715939459461121-D8FX

### Key Insights

- AI Overviews inflate impressions while suffocating clicks—cited sources marked as “position 1” create **visibility inflation without visitors**.
- Remaining clicks pack **serious purchase intent**—“pure protein” vs. empty-calorie traffic.
- SEO success now means echoing across **Google, ChatGPT, Reddit, YouTube, and TikTok**—the full orchestra of search touchpoints.
- New KPIs: share-of-voice, visibility, influence—**rank alone is obsolete**.
- ChatGPT overtaking Google’s traffic pushed to ~2030 if current trends persist (updated prediction).

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

Production KPIs must expand beyond rankings and clicks to **share-of-voice across AI and social platforms**. Content calendars should orchestrate multi-channel presence—one blog post is insufficient without Reddit, YouTube, and TikTok amplification.
