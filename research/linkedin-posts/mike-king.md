## Post 1: SEO in 2025 — Agents Over AI Content Writing

### Key Insights

- Most SEOs will keep doing mediocre content, volume link building, and manual processes—**the opportunity is for those who adopt agents**.
- GenAI’s value in SEO goes **far beyond writing content**; many practitioners haven’t adopted its true analytical and workflow potential.

## Post 2: AI Mode Patents — Why It’s Not “Just SEO”

**Date:** ~June 2025 (posted 12 months before June 16, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/michaelkingphilly_how-ai-mode-and-ai-overviews-work-based-on-activity-7335330568374571008-ik6d  
**Related resource:** https://searchengineland.com/

### Key Insights

- AI Mode and AI Overviews operate on patent-documented systems including **query fan-out**—multiple synthetic sub-queries power a single AI answer.
- SEO has always been about search as an internet function; as the market fractures again, **Relevance Engineering** becomes the strategic frame—not traditional keyword SEO alone.
- The C-suite undervalues SEO partly due to the Dunning-Kruger effect; search is a **business strategy question** requiring executive representation.
- Whether called SEO, AEO, GEO, or LLMO, the question is how websites and brands approach search across fragmented surfaces.
- Blocking generative AI crawlers is a strategic mistake—visibility requires being readable by AI systems.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

Content must cover **sub-query clusters** that query fan-out generates, not single keywords. Production briefs should map fan-out topics and ensure content addresses the cross-section of passages AI systems compete against for citation.

## Post 3: Embeddings Index for AIO/AI Mode Competitive Analysis

**Date:** ~June 2025 (posted 12 months before June 16, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/michaelkingphilly_ive-been-thinking-about-how-valuable-an-activity-7333539956411322370-JJW3

### Key Insights

- An **embeddings index of the web** would reveal which passages AI Overviews cite and which keywords those URLs rank for.
- Method: extract citation chunks from AIO responses → find nearest neighbors in embeddings index → isolate topical cross-sections in rankings data.
- This produces a ranked list of **passages you compete against** by semantic similarity—not just URL-level competitors.
- Teams could reverse-engineer AI reasoning by simulating why specific passages were chosen using Gemini.
- Semantic-level visibility into AI outputs plus rankings data could reshape SEO competitive analysis entirely.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

Introduces a production intelligence loop: before writing, analyze **which semantic passages AI already cites** for target queries and engineer content to compete at the passage level. Content should be structured in extractable chunks with high fact density matching cited passage patterns.

## Post 5: Tracking AI Overview Traffic in GA4

**Date:** ~May 2025 (posted 13 months before June 16, 2026)  
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/michaelkingphilly_how-to-track-traffic-from-ai-overviews-aio-activity-7308889507938877441-BoG5  
**Related resource:** https://www.kpplaybook.com/ (Dana DiTomaso GA4 guide)

### Key Insights

- Tracking traffic from AI Overviews, Featured Snippets, and PAA in GA4 requires **specialized filtering**—standard channel reports miss SERP feature traffic.
- Dana DiTomaso’s KP Playbook provides methods to segment AIO, snippet, and PAA referral patterns in GA4.
- `#:~:text=` URL fragments from highlight links can skew direct vs. organic attribution—workarounds needed in Looker/reporting.
- Without proper measurement, teams cannot evaluate whether AI content strategies produce real traffic impact.
- AI visibility work needs the same measurement granularity traditional SEO tools provide.

### Relevance to AI-Powered SEO Content Production

Closes the loop on content ROI: production teams must implement **GA4 tracking for AI referral sources** before scaling content. Without measurement, AI content investment cannot be justified or optimized.
